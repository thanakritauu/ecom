Payment-method marks (trademarks of their owners; used to identify the accepted payment methods).

- `visa.svg`, `mastercard.svg` — aaronfagan/svg-credit-card-payment-icons, `flat-rounded/` (Apache-2.0),
  https://github.com/aaronfagan/svg-credit-card-payment-icons — fetched 2026-10-04, unmodified.
- `promptpay.png` — Wikimedia Commons "File:PromptPay-logo.png" (Public domain), fetched 2026-10-04, unmodified.
- `truemoney.png` — Wikimedia Commons "File:True Money Logo.png" (CC BY 4.0), fetched 2026-10-04;
  cropped to the wordmark and white made transparent.
- `square/{promptpay,truemoney,visa,mastercard,ktb,scb,krungsri,kplus,make}.png` — official square app/brand icons
  provided by the client (2026-10-08), 80×80 with the rounded corners baked in; used unmodified. They replaced the
  temporary in-house approximations (`promptpay-square.svg`, `truemoney-square.svg`, `banks/*.svg`, now deleted;
  the generic `mobilebanking.svg` phone mark was dropped too — the bank-app method shows its bank icons instead).
  Wired through `METHOD_ICONS` / `PAYMENT_BANKS` in `src/components/checkout/paymentMarks.ts`.
